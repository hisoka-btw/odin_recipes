Beef Samosas

Description

Beef samosas are a popular South Asian snack featuring a crispy, golden-brown pastry filled with a deliciously seasoned beef mixture. To prepare them, start by heating oil in a pan and adding finely chopped onions, ginger-garlic paste, green chilies, and ground beef. Cook the mixture until the beef is browned and fully cooked, then add salt, turmeric, red chili powder, cumin, coriander powder, and garam masala. Stir well to combine the spices and allow the mixture to cook until most of the moisture evaporates. Finally, add chopped coriander leaves and let the filling cool completely. Prepare the pastry dough using all-purpose flour, a little salt, oil or ghee, and water. Knead until smooth, cover, and allow it to rest for approximately 20–30 minutes. To assemble the samosas, divide the dough into small portions, roll each portion into a thin oval shape, and cut it in half. Fold each half into a cone, seal the edge with a little flour-and-water paste, and fill it with the prepared beef mixture. Close the open edge firmly to create a triangular shape, ensuring there are no gaps. Heat oil in a deep frying pan over medium-low heat and carefully fry the samosas until they become crispy and golden brown on all sides. Remove them and place them on paper towels to absorb excess oil. Serve the freshly prepared beef samosas hot with mint chutney, tamarind sauce, or tomato ketchup.

Ingredients

  • 2 large potatoes
  • 1 cup frozen peas
  • 2 tablespoons vegetable oil
  • 1/2 teaspoons cumin seeds
  • 1 bay leaf
  • 1 pound ground beef
  • 2 large onions
  • 4 cloves garlic
  • 1 tablespoon minced fresh ginger root
  • 1 and 1/2 teaspoons salt
  • 1 teaspoon ground cumin
  • 1 teaspoon ground coriander
  • 1 teaspoon ground turmeric
  • 1 teaspoon chili powder

Steps

  1. Bring a medium saucepan of lightly salted water to a boil. Stir in potatoes and peas; cook until potatoes are tender but still firm, about 15 minutes. Drain, mash together, and set aside.
  2. Meanwhile, heat 2 tablespoons oil in a large saucepan over medium-high heat. Add cumin seeds and bay lead; stir until fragrant and seeds are browned. Add ground beef and onions; cook until beef is browned and crumbly and onions are soft, about 5 to 7 minutes.
  3. Stir in garlic and ginger.Season with salt, cumin, coriander, turmeric, chili powder, cinnamon, cardamom, and black pepper. Stir in the mashed potato mixture. Remove from heat and chill in the refrigerator for 1 hour, or until cool.
  4. Heat 1 quart of oil in a deep saucepan over medium heat.
  5. Mix cilantro and green chile peppers into potato and beef mixture. Place approximately 1 tablespoon of the mixture onto each phyllo sheet. Fold sheets into triangles, pressing edges together with moistened fingers.
  6. Lower samosas carefully into the hot oil in batches. Fry until golden brown, about 3 minutes. Transfer to a paper towel-lined plate to drain. Repeat with remaining samosas. Serve warm.
